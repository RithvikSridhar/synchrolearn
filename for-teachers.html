What We Look For

We're looking for passionate educators — not just subject experts

  • Qualified & Experienced in Your Subject

    A degree, certification, or professional experience in your teaching area.

  • Passionate About Teaching

    You genuinely enjoy helping students learn and grow. That shows in every session.

  • Available for At Least 5 Hours/Week

    Consistency matters. Even a few hours a week can make a meaningful income.

  • Good Internet & Equipment

    Stable broadband, webcam, and a quiet space for distraction-free sessions.

Don't worry if you don't tick all boxes — apply anyway! We value potential and passion just as much as credentials.
